# Core 14 font metrics

`core14.py` is generated from the Adobe Core 14 AFM files (Courier, Helvetica,
Times, Symbol, and ZapfDingbats families). Adobe distributes those files with
the notice that they "may be used, copied, and distributed for any purpose and
without charge, with or without modification, provided that all copyright
notices are retained; that the AFM files are not distributed without this file;
that the files may not be sold or modified for sale; and that Adobe is credited".
The glyph widths and font properties here are that data, restated as Python
literals. Copyright (c) 1985, 1987, 1989, 1990, 1991, 1992, 1993, 1997 Adobe
